Missing form policy, per form

Today one segmented control decides what happens for every form on the door. A liability waiver and a marketing opt-in do not deserve the same answer. Five ways to move the decision onto the form, each shown in the two surfaces it has to live in: onboarding step 4 and Door settings › Forms. In all five, the toggle connects the form to the door — a form that isn't collected there has no policy to set.

01

Per-form switch, inline

Recommended

Why · The decision belongs to the form, so it sits on the form's row. Nothing new to learn — the control users already know, three times.

Trade-off · Three controls to read instead of one. Past six or seven forms the column turns into noise, and there is no way to see the door's overall stance.

02

Three levels of strictness

Recommended

Why · Studios want a middle setting: let the member train, but stop the pass at the desk so someone actually collects the signature. Flagging alone gets ignored in the log.

Trade-off · A third option has to be explained, and "flag it" versus "sign at desk" is a fine distinction to weigh during setup.
