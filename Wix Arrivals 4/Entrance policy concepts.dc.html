TURN 1 · SETTINGS ONLY
Entrance policy: three ways to keep one default simple and let power users add more
A policy = check-in window (X min before / X min after session start) + intake forms. One default policy, connected to all doors. Each concept shows the Doors & access index and a single door page. Doors, hardware and plans cards are unchanged from v5 and collapsed here.
1aPolicy card on the index, doors pick from a dropdown
The default policy is edited in one place on Doors & access. Each door has a dropdown that says "Default policy". The only way to create a second policy is "Create new policy…" at the bottom of that dropdown, so most users never see policy management at all.
Trade-off: the door page doesn't show the rules themselves, only a summary.

1bEdit at the door, shared until you split it
The word "policy" barely appears. The door page keeps the full editor from v5 (window + forms), with a badge saying the rules are shared by all doors. Editing changes every door. One link, "Make this door different", forks a policy just for that door.
Trade-off: users must notice the "shared" badge or they may change every door by accident.

1cPolicies tab, doors assigned to policies
The architecture is visible: Doors & access gets a second tab, Entrance policies. It lists one default policy with its doors as chips and a "+ New policy" button. Each policy is a card you edit in place; doors are assigned from the policy, not the door.
Trade-off: most users see a tab and a list with one item they never need to touch.
