TURN 1 · LEAN V1 ONBOARDING
Five ways to set up one entrance and the plans that open it
v1 asks for two things only: an entrance and the plans connected to it. No entry rules, intake forms, member pass or hardware. Every concept ends on Live arrivals. They differ in sequence, density, defaults, mental model and where the setup lives.
1aTwo steps, one decision each
The v7 wizard cut to its two remaining steps. Step 1 names the entrance and picks a location. Step 2 switches on the plans that open it. Same shell, same stepper, so nothing else in the product changes.
Trade-off: a stepper for two steps reads as ceremony, and users may expect more steps to appear.

Trade-off: less room to explain what connecting a plan means; the helper text has to do that work.

1cAlready set up. Just confirm.
On install, Arrivals creates "Main entrance" at the business address and connects every active plan. Onboarding is a review of what was done, with inline edits. The only required action is one click.
Trade-off: the default is permissive. Owners with a plan that shouldn't grant entry (Off-peak, a workshop pass) must notice it and switch it off.

1dStart from the plans you already sell
The question is inverted: not "which plans open this door" but "which of my plans include entry". The screen is the Pricing Plans list with one new column. The entrance is implied, named "Front entrance", and renamed inline in the sentence above the list.
Trade-off: a business with two entrances on day one has to add the second in settings afterwards.

1eNo wizard. The dashboard asks.
Install lands straight on Live arrivals. The empty state is a two-item checklist; each item opens a small panel over the dashboard and ticks off when done. Nothing blocks, and the user is already in the place they will come back to every day.
Trade-off: some users leave half-done, so the dashboard needs a persistent "setup incomplete" state until both items are ticked.
