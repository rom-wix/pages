Five ways to manage areas, entrances and plans on one page
Same data in every concept: two locations, four areas, five entrances, four plans. Plans attach to areas, hardware to entrances. v1 scope, so no entry rules. The concepts differ in what the page is organised around, how much shows at once, and where editing happens.
1aExpandable area cards
One card per area. The header is the summary; expanding shows entrances on the left and plans on the right. Names rename in place, entrances are added inline, hardware connects from the row. This is what v10 does.
Trade-off: the page gets long with many areas, and comparing plans across areas means opening several cards.

1bTree on the left, detail on the right
A rail shows Location › Area › Entrance as a tree. Selecting an area fills the right pane with its plans and entrances; selecting an entrance swaps the pane to its hardware. The whole hierarchy is visible at once and the page never changes.
Trade-off: an unusual pattern for a Wix dashboard page, and it needs the full width to breathe.

1cPlan matrix
Areas as rows, plans as columns, a toggle in every cell. "Which plan gets in where" is one glance. Each row expands to show its entrances and hardware underneath.
Trade-off: entrances and hardware feel secondary, and the grid stops fitting past five or six plans.

1dTable plus side drawer
A dense table of areas: location, entrances, hardware, plans. Clicking a row opens a drawer on the right with that area's entrances and plan toggles. The list stays in view while you edit, so you can hop between areas.
Trade-off: a drawer is one more layer, and renaming happens in the drawer rather than in the list.

1eLocation tabs, one open form
Tabs switch location. Below, every area in that location is a section with its entrances and plan chips laid out flat. Nothing collapses, nothing opens; you scroll and edit. Plans are chips you toggle by clicking, which keeps each area to a few lines.
Trade-off: chips hide plan details like subscribers and hours, and a location with eight areas is a long scroll.
