Direction 2 · optional satellite

WTI settlement momentum, paper-traded first

Rule
At 14:00 NY compute s = ln(price just before 09:30 ÷ prior session's 14:29–14:30 price). If |s| is above its trailing 250-session 80th percentile, buy (s > 0) or sell (s < 0) XTIUSD at market, and exit at market at 14:29–14:30. Roughly 55 trades a year, flat by the close, so no swap.
Evidence
2005–2020 minute data: Sharpe 0.88 before 2013 and 0.53 after, 9.4 bp net per trade. It has earned money mainly in high-volatility years, which describes the last 12 months, but it hasn't been tested on them yet.
Go / no-go
Run it only if your measured spread plus slippage at 14:00 and 14:30 NY is 3 bp a side or less (breakeven is 7.7 bp). Trade fixed notional; volatility targeting made it worse. Stop it if realised slippage drifts above the model, not on P&L alone.
